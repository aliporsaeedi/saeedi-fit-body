# Coach catalog v2

The explicitly supplied ZIP was inspected without running its Python, bot or GIF scripts. Five XLSX files were inspected, including the 90-sheet master. There are no Word documents in this archive. All 9,164 formula occurrences were independently evaluated using a bounded parser for the actual functions present; no broken references or cached numeric discrepancies were found. Thirty-two missing formula caches in the master were calculated. Duplicate workbook copies are included in this formula-occurrence count.

753 normalized records were imported into seven additive Google Sheets tabs and the isolated native D1 catalog. Names, original source IDs, goals, audiences and level fields are preserved where provided. Default templates preserve the supplied training, nutrition and seven-slot supplement columns. The original 963,689-byte ZIP is stored as 41 reconstructable archive parts; its SHA-256 is `3588319cdb87b9b53a93482634039531c5f0561b671b154b820705d2090aa5aa`.

Record kinds: 199 foods, 38 recipes, 8 nutrition styles, 43 diet templates, 17 workout templates, 382 source exercise definitions, 25 supplements, 6 supplement charts, 28 substitutions, 1 intake definition and 6 source calculators. Exercise definitions from two source versions are retained separately by source ID; this is not a claim of 382 new unique active movements or new GIFs. The existing live exercise bank snapshot has 2,141 active rows, 2,128 rows with an instruction reference and 1,329 with a GIF reference. Media-reference counts do not prove every remote URL works.

Every initially written Sheet cell and every imported D1 column was read back. The original archive reconstructs byte-for-byte. Twenty-one source-derived clarifications then added original per-100g figures to the old diet editor and explicit injury/replacement references to home workouts; names, portions, nutrients and source bytes were unchanged and read back again.

Forty review records retain actual findings: six energy-versus-simple-4/4/9 discrepancies requiring source/fiber review, eight clarified dry-weight household units, nineteen cyclic replacement chains handled with a visited-set stop, three original target-versus-total discrepancies and four templates outside their style's declared automatic-generation range. These are not all confirmed nutritional errors. No invented corrections to source nutrients, yield weights or dosage were applied. New home-workout exercise rows have no GIFs in the supplied archive; no media was fabricated.

The pure coaching engine implements the supplied adult energy/activity/goal rules, performance-score thresholds, injury-chain termination, constrained 3×3 nutrition scaling and explicit rounding/error checks. Of the 32 supplied style/energy pairs, 28 fit the declared automatic-generation ranges and were recalculated within the calorie tolerance; four remain review items rather than silently widening the author's ranges. Clinical cases and minors are not assigned an adult automatic calorie target. Supplement records remain coach review material, not an automatic prescription.

`PackCatalog.js` supplies staff-only catalog lookup, CAS program editing with a previous-version backup, assigned-coach scope checks, append-only owned assessments, and owned shopping-list calculations from registered grams and recipe yield. Shopping rows without a recorded gram quantity remain explicitly unresolved. The primary student database is still Google Sheets; this D1 catalog is a migration preview and does not automatically synchronize later coach edits.

Private source files, prepared tables, formula evaluation evidence and import receipts are outside this public repository. No real athlete data or configured credentials are included in test fixtures. The catalog manifest was activated after both stores were read back. All 66 program templates pass runtime structure validation. Two original gym templates use day headings rather than session headings; the parser now restores their 42 and 25 exercise rows. Explicit rest days survive assignment, later editing and native projection. One source named as a six-day plan contains only three days; its exact name is retained with a visible completeness warning and it is excluded from automatic recommendations. Templates without a declared level are also excluded from automatic ranking until reviewed.
